Partnership Structure

A 90-day pilot to earn a longer partnership.

Approach

Our objective is to improve forecast accuracy and increase revenue and gross margin. We begin with a 90-day sprint, or pilot, built around defined deliverables and outcomes — designed to establish trust, verify execution, and confirm a productive working partnership. Only once that trust is established does the client have the option to extend the engagement for an additional 9–12 months, scope dependent. This structure allows both parties to establish the working relationship before any longer-term commitment.

Our objective on every engagement is to automate Pemetic out of the role within 12–15 months, leaving the process and automation in place to sustain growth, eliminate creep and regression, and optimize SG&A.
