Exhibit A
Method

Process first. AI second. In that order, every time.

Most commercial teams don’t have an AI problem — they have a process problem AI can’t fix by itself. We install the qualification, pipeline, and workflow discipline that makes your data trustworthy, then push your existing CRM as far as it goes, buy point tools where it falls short, and build custom AI only where nothing else reaches — always with a human signing off wherever the output has to be court- or audit-grade.

Engagement

Try before you buy.

Every engagement starts as a short, paid pilot — not a multi-year contract. We quantify your baseline, install the process and the AI layer underneath it, and keep a monthly value scoreboard: fees against realized dollars.

If the savings don’t clearly beat the cost, we don’t extend. Our stated goal on every engagement is to automate ourselves out within 12–15 months — we’re not selling permanent overhead.

Exhibit D
Record

Two engagements, anonymized.

Capital-intensive platform · long enterprise cycles

Six-figure deals were stalling in late stage, with no clear reason.

Senior people were being pulled into every deal with no way to tell which ones were worth their time. We installed a real qualification standard — champion mapping, economic-buyer identification, explicit win-probability — then layered in AI-assisted deal review and drafting. Multiple stalled deals unblocked; the same team, moving faster.

Takeaway

The ROI wasn’t a flashy model — it was process discipline made durable by AI, so scarce senior time compounds instead of leaking into dead deals.

Lean team · time-boxed demand window

A small go-to-market team, flying blind through a demand surge.

Most open deals had no identified economic buyer and no scheduled next step — the team was working from memory, not from the pipeline. We fixed the CRM fundamentals first, then layered in a small suite of purpose-built AI agents covering the research and drafting work reps couldn’t get to by hand. Pipeline completeness rose sharply, and the team covered far more ground than headcount would suggest.

Takeaway

Custom AI agents let a lean team punch above its weight — but only after the data underneath is trustworthy. Agents on dirty pipeline data just automate the confusion.
