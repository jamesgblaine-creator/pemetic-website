Problems identified

Six-figure deals stalled late in the sales process. Inconsistent qualification and incomplete CRM records obscured deal risk, while senior leaders were pulled into every opportunity.

Mapping the solution

We joined live deal reviews and audited the pipeline to locate the bottlenecks. The sequence: establish qualification standards, define requirements for each sales stage, then automate recurring work.

Execution

  1. Added a CRM scorecard for champions, economic buyers, and win probability to focus senior attention on qualified deals.
  2. Introduced AI-assisted deal reviews and follow-up drafts to surface risks and speed next steps.
  3. Deployed a contracting tool to clear the late-stage legal bottleneck.

Problems identified

Before the engagement, the initial pipeline audit found that only 37% of open deals had an identified economic buyer; just 1 of 94 had a scheduled next step. A lean team could not manually cover demand within a limited window.

Mapping the solution

The audit established two priorities: make the CRM reliable, then automate work the team could not reach. We mapped the gaps to five workflows: outreach, deal review, pricing, legal, and pipeline intelligence.

Execution

  1. Standardized CRM fields, deal qualification, sales-stage requirements, and scheduled next steps.
  2. Deployed five AI agents for account research and outreach, deal health checks, indicative pricing, contract redline support, and forecast hygiene.
  3. Maintained the CRM data standard as the agents extended account coverage.
