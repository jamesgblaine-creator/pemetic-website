Our Work

Two engagements, anonymized.

01

Complex, multi-stakeholder sales cycles

Six-figure deals were stalling in late stage, with no clear reason.

Senior people were pulled into every deal with no way to tell which ones were worth their time. We installed a real qualification standard — champion mapping, economic-buyer identification, explicit win-probability — then layered in AI-assisted deal review. Multiple stalled deals unblocked, same team, moving faster.

Takeaway

The ROI wasn’t a flashy model — it was process discipline made durable by AI, so scarce senior time compounds instead of leaking into dead deals.

Lean team · time-boxed demand window

A small go-to-market team, flying blind through a demand surge.

Most open deals had no identified economic buyer and no scheduled next step — the team was working from memory, not the pipeline. We fixed the CRM fundamentals first, then added a small suite of purpose-built AI agents for the research and drafting reps couldn’t get to by hand. Pipeline completeness rose sharply; the team covered far more ground than headcount would suggest.

Takeaway

Custom AI agents let a lean team punch above its weight — but only after the data underneath is trustworthy. Agents on dirty pipeline data just automate the confusion.
