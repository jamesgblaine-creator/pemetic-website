Case Studies

Two client engagements, presented in anonymized form.

01

Complex, multi-stakeholder sales cycles

Six-figure, late-stage opportunities were stalling without identifiable cause.

Senior leadership was engaged on every opportunity, with no mechanism to distinguish which merited that attention. We implemented a formal qualification standard — champion mapping, economic-buyer identification, and explicit win-probability scoring — then introduced AI-assisted deal review. Multiple stalled, late-stage opportunities were unblocked and advanced within the 90-day pilot, with no increase in headcount.

Takeaway

The return was not a projection model; it was process discipline made durable by AI, allowing scarce senior capacity to compound rather than dissipate across unqualified opportunities.

Lean team · time-boxed demand window

A lean go-to-market team lacked pipeline visibility during a period of peak demand.

~1 in 3open deals with an identified economic buyer
<5%open deals with a scheduled next step

The team was operating from memory rather than the pipeline of record. We addressed CRM data integrity first, then deployed a small suite of purpose-built AI agents to handle research and drafting work the team could not reach manually. Pipeline completeness improved substantially within the pilot period, and the team covered materially more account volume than headcount would suggest.

Takeaway

Custom AI agents allow a lean team to operate beyond its headcount — but only once the underlying data can be trusted. Agents layered on unreliable pipeline data simply automate the confusion.
