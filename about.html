About

Jake Blaine

Bio

Jake Blaine is founder and President of Pemetic Partners, where he helps growth-stage energy, infrastructure, and industrial companies improve commercial execution. His work draws on more than 20 years of experience building and scaling businesses.

Before founding Pemetic, Jake held leadership roles at Aspen Power, PearlX, Stem, and EnerNOC, working on the development, financing, ownership, and operation of renewable energy and energy-efficiency asset portfolios. He co-founded PearlX and served as Executive Vice President at Aspen Power.

Jake began his career in Enterprise Rent-A-Car’s management training program before joining Bloomberg as a commodities analyst. He later served as Executive Director of Tompkins Conservation, helping create more than 10 million acres of national parks in Chile and Argentina.

Jake serves as a Director of Pacolet Milliken and a Board Observer at Milliken & Company.
